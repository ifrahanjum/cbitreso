# Project: reso CBIT Academic Resource Hub

## Context
A centralized platform for CBIT students and faculty to upload, organize, and search academic resources. Consolidates materials from scattered sources like CloudFront, SPDC, and Google Drive.

## Creative North Star: "The Digital Curator"
- **Aesthetic**: Premium, editorial, breathable.
- **Philosophy**: Content as the hero. No boxes or harsh lines.
- **Design System**: "Cura Editorial" (Indigo accents, Inter font, tonal layering).

## Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: Vue 3 (Composition API), Vite, Tailwind CSS, Pinia, Vue Router.
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express.
- **Database/Auth/Storage**: Supabase.

## Stakeholders
- CBIT Students (Primary Users)
- CBIT Faculty (Uploaders/Advisors)
- Admin (Moderators)
